Months & Reports

The month-end stocktake, start to finish
How a month works: the month opens by itself on the 29th → the shop floor count in Monthly Stocktake → you check the figures and download the report (step 2) → you finish the month (step 3), which emails you the figures for the accountant.

1. Start a month (usually not needed)

Each month opens automatically on the 29th (27th/28th in February) and stays open until the 5th of the next month. Only use this to open one at another time, or if one is missing. Counters can only count a month that's open.
Exchange rates are copied from last month - only change them if they've moved.

2. Check the figures and get the report

Use this once the counting is done. Pick the month, compare it with an earlier one, and download the Excel report.
Downloads an Excel file with a Summary tab (glass, consumables, WIP and totals against the comparison month) and a Full List tab with every item, the movement, and anything worth checking highlighted.
Changed a price after the count? Update it in SGP Products, then press this. It re-prices the counted items for this month (open months only) and shows you every change first.

3. Finish the month

Locks the month so no more counts can go in, and emails you the figures and the report. You'll need the bi-annual consumables figure. You can reopen a month if you need to.

Past months

Every month, its totals and its saved report. The reports are also in Teams: Files → Stock & Purchasing → Stocktake reports.

Monthly figures overview

A year at a glance - glass, consumables, WIP and the rest-of-consumables figure for every month, April to March, with the change on the month before. Same layout as your Monthly Tracking sheet.

Settings and emails

Who gets each email (weekly sheets, month-end summary, problem reports), the counter sign-ins, and who gets the weekly glass and consumables sheets, and which items are counted each week with their minimums (download the list, change it, import it back). The weekly check is for stock levels only - it never goes on the accountant's report.

Backups

A copy of every list the app uses (products, suppliers, POs, counts, weekly levels) as one Excel file in Teams: Files → Stock & Purchasing → Backups. One is taken automatically every time a month is finished.

Go-live: clear out the test data

Deletes every test PO and PO line, every month and stock count line, and every weekly count - so the real November count and PO SGP 23850 start clean. Keeps SGP Products, Suppliers, Checkers and your settings. Only shows in the test app.
